One visual grammar

Screen brackets retain the display identity. The crossing recalls Mirax's X. Every stroke ends and joins round; the smaller system mark opens up for legibility.

Adaptive-safe

The foreground uses a 108dp canvas. All visible strokes sit inside the centered 66dp safe circle. Launcher studies show the central 72dp mask window; the square master keeps the full canvas.

Assets vs. illustration

All six glyphs use the exact Android path data. Masks, tints, tile states, bars and overlay surfaces are illustrative, not additional assets or implemented settings. No external resources are loaded.
