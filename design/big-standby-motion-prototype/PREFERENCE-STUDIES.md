## Preference hypotheses

1. **Strong: attached, coherent contour anatomy.** 17 and all three of its 72/73/74 descendants are kept. Their shared traits are precise fine lines, real X registration, connected corner junctions and local shape changes. Ten new studies deliberately explore this cluster rather than another equal split across every source.
2. **Moderate: thin sections, not an independent volumetric machine.** 14 and 51 are kept, but 69-71 and 87-89 are rejected. This supports trying smaller straight internal sections while avoiding large curved beams, deep shells, hinged windows and broad crossing slabs. It does not prove the exact rejection reason.
3. **Moderate: small pressure facets, not global mechanical torque.** 20 is kept and 77 is rejected; 75/76 are still unrated. Two proposals return to narrow edge-attached facets, without treating the unrated siblings as endorsed.
4. **Mixed: specific lozenge spatial grammar, not any animated net.** 36/45 remain kept while all 81-86 descendants are rejected. Four bounded experiments retain the originals shared-edge rhombi, small face assembly and local elevation/topology; avoid flat triangular meshes, sweeping domains and dense general-purpose wireframes. These are the exploratory minority.
5. **Strong constraint, uncertain causation: the actual X stays fixed.** All previous X-body motion candidates were rejected. Keep the hollow double-outline X, centre diamond, typography, asymmetric plates, shared masks and tiny OLED shift. Do not add particles, strobe, full-ring breathing, detached sticks or long inactive pauses.
6. **Moderate: subtle contact, not branching circuitry.** 58 is kept, 90/91 rejected and 92 unrated. One proposal tests a tiny contour-attached contact instead of another large Y network.

The user explicitly requested richer particles for theme C, not D. C preferences do not authorize particles or stronger glow in D. The C module, controls and appearance are outside this change.
