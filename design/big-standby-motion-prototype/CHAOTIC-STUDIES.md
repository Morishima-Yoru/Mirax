# D motion studies — CHAOTIC-origin exploration

The 30 concepts numbered 119–148 were generated by the requested CHAOTIC
specialist before implementation was assigned to Designer. Their original,
individually specified briefs are preserved in `CHAOTIC-BRIEFS.json`.
The concepts are experiments, not judgments about the user's taste.

The preceding cleanup removed only recorded rejections and retained both kept
and unrated candidates. Historical votes and written notes remain local data;
new studies do not inherit either. A background may use meaningful structure.
The user subsequently clarified that prior CPU-load feedback was specific to
one study, not a general limit.
Fixed-track reversals with easing at the endpoints remain avoided.

## Implementation adaptations

- **119:** The scanning band excludes the X body instead of filling it opaque,
  preserving the host's fixed hollow double outline.
- **120:** The slat mask is an intersection with the complement of the X,
  not an even-odd symmetric difference that would fill areas between slats.
- **123, 124 and 135:** Mechanics can be positioned in the visible field or
  referenced to an X direction because original arm coordinates may lie
  entirely outside a portrait viewport.
- **127:** The three distinct slides deliberately hold for six seconds each;
  they are not intended to evolve every second.
- **143:** The spirit-level indicator moves forward in a loop instead of
  sliding back and forth with endpoint easing.

The D host, controls, review panel and Note feature remain shared. The earlier
batch used per-frame operation and paint limits as proxies. Those limits were
later removed after the user clarified that CPU bounds were not a
general requirement; prior notes remain historical evidence, not a rule.
